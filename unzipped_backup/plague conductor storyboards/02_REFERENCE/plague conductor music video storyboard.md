# PLAGUE CONDUCTOR - Music Video Storyboard

## Video Overview
A dark, visceral metal music video following the Plague Conductor as he orchestrates the sacrifice of convicted criminals to save innocent villages from zombie hordes. Visual style: gritty, apocalyptic, with cinematic horror mixed with symphonic imagery.

**Video Length**: ~2:55 (full duration of song)  
**Total Scenes**: 90 detailed scenes  
**Average Cut Duration**: ~2 seconds (standard for high-energy metal videos)  
**Visual Style**: Dark, gothic, symphonic, cinematic horror with strategic moral complexity

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep blacks, dark grays, sickly greens, blood reds
- **Accents**: Sickly yellow (plague/disease), electric blues (danger), gold (orchestral elements)
- **Transitions**: Often through fog, smoke, or shadows

- **Close-ups**: Emotional moments with prisoners and conductor

- **Lighting**: High contrast, dramatic shadows, occasional lightning flashes

- **Conductor's Baton**: Glows/trails during key moments, represents control
- **Fog/Mist**: Represents plague and death
- **Chains**: Represent both imprisonment and connection/fate
- **Light**: Villages = safety and innocence, contrasts with darkness

### Tone
- Dark and apocalyptic
- Morally complex (not glorifying violence, but showing necessary sacrifice)
- Symphonic and controlled (despite chaos)
- Theatrical and metal-inspired
- Cinematic and professional
